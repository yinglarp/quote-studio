# Menu and dietary notes

Prepared for the GA200 course. In a real business, the kitchen would confirm every line here.

## Kitchen

- Food is prepared in a halal-certified central kitchen in Senoko. Every dish is halal.
- The kitchen handles nuts, shellfish and gluten, so any dish can pick up traces of them. **No dish is guaranteed free of peanuts or any other allergen.**
- A dish may be described as free of an allergen only when the table below says it contains no such ingredient, and even then the reply must mention the shared kitchen.

## What the Menu agent does with this

- A guest with an allergy to an ingredient in the table is a clash with every package that includes that dish.
- A guest who is vegetarian can eat only the dishes marked Yes. Count them for the package asked for.
- The menu file doesn't say whether a dish can be made without an ingredient. Only Priya, after checking with Chef Hafiz, can offer a changed menu.
