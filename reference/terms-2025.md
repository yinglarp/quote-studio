# Heicoders Catering: Terms and Conditions 2025

Effective 1 January 2025. These terms replace the 2024 terms.
Approved by Siti Rahman, Managing Director.

These terms apply to every order placed with Heicoders Catering Pte Ltd for delivery in Singapore.

## 1. Quotes and confirmation

1.1. A quote shows the package price per pax, the headcount, any add-ons, the delivery charge and GST.

1.2. An order is confirmed when we receive a deposit of 30% of the total (including GST).

1.3. The balance is invoiced after the event and is due within 14 days of the invoice date.

## 2. Headcount changes

2.1. You may change the headcount by up to 10% until 5 working days before the event.

2.2. After that, headcount changes depend on kitchen availability.

## 3. Cancellation

3.1. If you cancel at least 5 working days before the event, we refund your deposit in full.

3.2. If you cancel 2 to 4 working days before the event, we refund 50% of the deposit.

3.3. If you cancel less than 2 working days before the event, no refund is given.

A working day is Monday to Friday, excluding Singapore public holidays.

## 4. Delivery and setup

4.1. We deliver within a 30-minute window agreed at confirmation.

4.2. Buffet setup is included. We collect equipment within four hours of the end of the event.

## 5. Food safety

5.1. Food is prepared in our halal-certified central kitchen in Senoko.

5.2. Buffet food should be consumed within three hours of delivery.
