# Heicoders Catering: Terms and Conditions 2026

Effective 1 January 2026. These terms replace the 2025 terms.
Approved by Siti Rahman, Managing Director.

These terms apply to every order placed with Heicoders Catering Pte Ltd for delivery in Singapore.

## 1. Quotes and confirmation

1.1. A quote shows the package price per pax, the headcount, any add-ons, the delivery charge and GST.

1.2. An order is confirmed when we receive a deposit of 30% of the total (including GST).

1.3. The balance is invoiced after the event and is due within 14 days of the invoice date.

## 2. Headcount changes

2.1. You may change the headcount by up to 10% until 3 working days before the event.

2.2. After that, increases depend on kitchen availability, and decreases are not refunded.

2.3. Please confirm every headcount change in writing (email or WhatsApp to our Sales and Events team).

## 3. Cancellation

3.1. If you cancel at least 3 working days before the event, we refund your deposit in full.

3.2. If you cancel 1 to 2 working days before the event, the deposit is forfeited.

3.3. If you cancel on the day of the event, the full order value is charged.

A working day is Monday to Friday, excluding Singapore public holidays.

## 4. Delivery and setup

4.1. We deliver within a 30-minute window agreed at confirmation.

4.2. Buffet setup is included. We collect equipment within four hours of the end of the event.

4.3. Delivery charges are set out in our current price list.

## 5. Food safety

5.1. Food is prepared in our halal-certified central kitchen in Senoko.

5.2. Buffet food should be consumed within three hours of delivery.

5.3. Our kitchen handles nuts, shellfish and gluten. Allergen information is available on request from our Sales and Events team.

## 6. General

6.1. These terms may be updated. The version in force on the date of your event applies.
