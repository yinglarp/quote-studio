# Heicoders Catering: Terms and Conditions 2027

These terms take effect on 1 January 2027 and replace the 2026 terms.

These terms apply to every order placed with Heicoders Catering Pte Ltd for delivery in Singapore.

## 1. Quotes and confirmation

1.1. A quote shows the package price per pax, the headcount, any add-ons, the delivery charge and GST.

1.2. An order is confirmed when we receive a deposit of 30% of the total (including GST).

1.3. The balance is invoiced after the event and is due within 14 days of the invoice date.

1.4. Quotes are prepared from the price list in force on the date of the event.

## 2. Headcount changes

2.1. You may change the headcount by up to 10% until 3 working days before the event.

2.2. After that, increases depend on kitchen availability, and decreases are not refunded.

2.3. Please confirm every headcount change in writing (email or WhatsApp to our Sales and Events team).

2.4. Headcount changes for events of more than 200 pax must be made at least 7 days before the event.

## 3. Deposits

3.1. Deposits are held against the confirmed order and are applied to the final invoice.

3.2. Deposits cannot be transferred to another customer.

3.3. Deposits are refunded to the account that paid them.

## 4. Cancellation

4.1. Cancellations made at least 7 days before the event receive a full deposit refund.

4.2. Cancellations made 1 to 6 days before the event forfeit the deposit.

4.3. Cancellations made on the day of the event are charged the full order value.

4.4. If you wish to cancel, tell our Sales and Events team in writing.

## 5. Delivery and setup

5.1. We deliver within a 30-minute window agreed at confirmation.

5.2. Buffet setup is included. We collect equipment within four hours of the end of the event.

5.3. Delivery charges are set out in our current price list.

## 6. Food safety

6.1. Food is prepared in our halal-certified central kitchen in Senoko.

6.2. Buffet food should be consumed within three hours of delivery.

6.3. Our kitchen handles nuts, shellfish and gluten. Allergen information is available on request from our Sales and Events team.

## 7. General

7.1. These terms may be updated. The version in force on the date of your event applies.
